Practical tips
  • Most hospitals require upfront payment without insurance — carry your policy number
  • Pharmacies sell most medications over the counter — try one first for mild stomach issues
  • Call the hospital directly, not 115 — they speak English and send their own ambulance
  • Typical ER visit: $80–$120 (IV + blood tests). Hospitalization: $200–$500/day
